A research and development community that develops and maintains eiviz.  
[FlowingSPDG](https://github.com/FlowingSPDG) / Shugo Kawamura chairs it and is the lead engineer on eiviz.  
OMT send/receive, encode/decode, and the vMix-compatible APIs in eiviz are built on crates published by Mikansei Laboratory.
