---
title: Overlays
description: DSK on a Mixing Unit’s Program
---

DSK, downstream key, vMix Overlay. Up to eight per [Mixing Unit](/eiviz/en/concepts/mixing-unit/). The source is a [Scene](/eiviz/en/concepts/scenes/); a raw Input cannot sit here — put it in a Scene first.

They land on Program after CUT or the T-bar has mixed. Overlay in the main chrome sets position and size; the toggles on the desk turn them on and off.
