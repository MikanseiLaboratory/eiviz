---
title: Scenes
description: A composite stacked from Inputs
---

Inputs stacked as layers. Close to an OBS Scene, except eiviz does not TAKE a Scene directly. Switching is Preview/Program on a [Mixing Unit](/eiviz/en/concepts/mixing-unit/).

Add one from Scenes. Edit sets position, size, opacity, and order. Audio Follow on a layer ties that Input’s sound to the picture.

Use it on a Mixing Unit bus, as an [Overlay](/eiviz/en/concepts/overlays/) source, on a [Multiview](/eiviz/en/concepts/multiviews/) tile, or as an Output source.
