---
title: Audio Auxs
description: Master, Headphone, and AUX buses
---

The internal mix is 48 kHz stereo. Master and Headphone are always there and cannot be removed. AUX buses A–H, up to eight. Same job as a console AUX, a mix-minus, or an ISO send.

A [Mixing Unit](/eiviz/en/concepts/mixing-unit/) chooses which bus it feeds. Follow tracks Preview/Program and the T-bar. Independent sends Program sources only and ignores the T-bar.

Hardware assignment is Audio Auxiliary in [Settings](/eiviz/en/introduction/settings/). Enabled keeps the bus in the mix with no device. Headphone can copy Master. Device detail is [Audio, ASIO, and related](/eiviz/en/features/outputs/audio/).
