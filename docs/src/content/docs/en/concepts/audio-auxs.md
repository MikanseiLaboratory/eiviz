description: Master, Headphone, and AUX buses

The internal mix is 48 kHz stereo. Master and Headphone are always there and cannot be removed.  
AUX buses A–H, up to eight. Same job as a console AUX, a mix-minus, or an ISO send.

## Mixing Unit send

A [Mixing Unit](/eiviz/en/concepts/mixing-unit/) chooses which bus it feeds.  
Follow tracks Preview/Program and the T-bar mix. Independent ignores the video switch and mixes Inputs assigned to that bus as they are.

## Device assignment

Hardware assignment is Audio Auxiliary in [Settings](/eiviz/en/introduction/settings/).  
Enabled keeps the bus mixing internally with no output device.  
Headphone copies Master makes the Headphone bus a duplicate of Master.

Device detail is in [Audio, ASIO, and related](/eiviz/en/features/outputs/audio/).
